In current western music working with chords or harmonies is one of the key characteristics. During the centuries different approaches were used. The meaning of the term **harmony** goes back to the Greek _harmonia,_ which had in ancient times primarily mathematical and philosophical connotations. From the seventeenth century until the present day the so called "triad-based music" has been dominant. The **theory of harmony** developed into a separate theoretical-practical discipline. The most important coding systems which evolved during the last centuries are:

- figured bass/thoroughbass
- degrees (roman numerals)
- coding of harmonic functions (in the german theoretical tradition)
