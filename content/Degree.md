In classical harmony the note of a scale on which a chord can be built.
Roman numerals indicate the triad on that degree consisting of scale notes.

Also Arabic numerals are used with a caret (^). This used in Schenker analysis to indicate important melodic lines.

In sources on Partimento bass notes are also indicated with encircled Arabic numerals. An example of this is the book by Job IJzerman: *Harmony, Counterpoint, Partimento: A New Method Inspired by Old Masters.*
